The execution gap

Value leaks between the decision and the shelf.

When strategy isn’t translated into simple store action, it never reaches the shopper. Most companies hold the data, write the strategy and present the slides, then lose the return somewhere between the boardroom and the shelf.

The decision

Strategy is rarely the problem.

Brand owners are well supplied with POS data, syndicated reports, supplier decks and category reviews.

The translation

Insight gets lost in translation.

Strategy and execution live in different silos. Insight rarely becomes a price tag, a planogram or a store action.

The shelf

Reality is decided on the shelf.

On-shelf availability, share and margin are confirmed in-store, not in the slide deck.

What it costs when the plan does not reach the shelf

ShoppersEmpty shelf, delayed purchases or alternatives chosen
BasketSmaller baskets, lost sales
Store KPIsLower satisfaction and footfall
BrandTrade spend under-delivery, brand switching

What is Kiyan?

Kiyan is a category management and retail execution consultancy based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. It works with manufacturers and retailers as an independent partner, taking strategy from the boardroom to the shelf through six pillars: Right Reach, Right Category, Right Range, Right Store, Right Space and Right Execution.

What are Kiyan’s six pillars?

Right Reach (retail census, store universe and coverage); Right Category (category roles and joint business planning); Right Range (range architecture and assortment productivity); Right Store (store clustering, segmentation and adjacency); Right Space (space-to-sales analysis and store-specific planograms); and Right Execution (AI shelf audit, on-shelf availability and gap-to-task closure).

Who does Kiyan work with?

Manufacturers and retailers who own the shelf, and the leaders responsible for it: commercial, sales, category, trade marketing and insight teams. Kiyan is headquartered in Riyadh and works across Saudi Arabia and the wider region.

What is the free pilot?

Kiyan offers a pilot at no cost and with no commitment: one store, one category, one week. You share the current planogram or realogram for the pilot store, product images and dimensions, and your category vision. Kiyan returns redesigned planogram scenarios with the justification, and supports implementation and evaluation.

How do I start working with Kiyan?

Confirm a pilot store and category for a free pilot, request a discovery call, or send your RFP to info@kiyan.sa.
