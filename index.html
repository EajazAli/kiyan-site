The execution gap

Strategy is rarely the problem. The shelf is where it leaks.

Brand owners already hold POS data, syndicated reports, supplier decks and category reviews. Strategy and execution live in different silos, so insight rarely becomes a price tag, a planogram or a store action. OSA, share and margin are confirmed in-store, not in the slide deck.

  • ShoppersEmpty shelf, delayed purchase or an alternative in the basket
  • BasketsSmaller baskets, lost sales
  • Store KPIsLower satisfaction and footfall
  • BrandTrade spend under-delivers, shoppers switch
