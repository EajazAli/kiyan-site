How we work

One table. Three roles. One shared objective.

Kiyan sits between manufacturer and retailer as the independent category management partner. We structure the project, integrate the data and manage it from assessment to pilot to scale.

Manufacturer

Brings the category vision.

  • Category vision
  • Shopper and consumer insights
  • Brand and market expertise
  • Data and analytics
  • Innovation
  • Investment
Kiyan · independent partner

Structures the work, end to end.

  • Category assessment
  • Data integration and analytics
  • Category workshops
  • Assortment, pricing and space
  • Execution roadmap and pilot design
  • Performance measurement
  • Project governance and scaling
Retailer

Brings the store reality.

  • POS and shopper data
  • Commercial priorities
  • Store and operational reality
  • Assortment and execution
  • Pilot and performance measurement
  • Expansion and scale
